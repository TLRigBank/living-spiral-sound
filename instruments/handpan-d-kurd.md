# Handpan — D Kurd family

Common 9-note layout:

`D3 (ding) / A3 Bb3 C4 D4 E4 F4 G4 A4`

10-note often adds `C5`. Extended pans add higher D/E/F.

## Language on Hadpan1

Available scale: D natural minor / Kurd.
Played language: **D, E, A** and their octaves.

| Degree | Role on this take |
|---|---|
| D3 ding | Downbeat stamp |
| D4 / D5 | Engine — octave bounce |
| A4 | Fifth, hovering center |
| E4 | The only color that moves with A |
| F, G, Bb, C | Almost unused as melody |

Arrangement rule: leave the pan hovering. Put F and Bb on piano, drone, or voice, and only in the breaths.

## Motif cells (counted from Hadpan1)

1. `D5 – D4 – D5`
2. `A4 – D5 – A4`
3. `E4 – A4 – E4`
